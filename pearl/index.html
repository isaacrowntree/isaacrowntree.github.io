Four decisions doing all the work

Everything here is chosen so the product sells itself at a market stall in four seconds, with a loud crowd and no pitch.

Collection

Cups, not stamps

A punch card is a chore. Twelve seasonal cups with an SSR drop is a compulsion — the same instinct that made Yo-Chi's golden ticket work.

Screenshot rate is the metric, not scan rate.

Word of mouth

One order, four stamps

Boba is a group purchase. One person carries four drinks, so one scan credits four people. That's four signups from one order.

The run is already social — the app just has to notice. And every one of the four still pours six paid drinks before their own freebie.

First visit

The first drink is free

Not "collect six and get one." Scan, and there's a free drink waiting right now. The offer replaces the pitch entirely.

One QR, three words: Free boba. Scan. The launch freebie is on PEARL — never on the shop.

Hard limit

Three seconds flat

Confetti fires optimistically; the ledger settles after. If it slows the line, staff quietly stop mentioning it and the network dies in six weeks.

Confetti is free. Latency never is.

If it's your counter · 给老板的话

先卖出六杯，才送一杯 —— 送出那杯的店，迎来一位带着朋友的新客。

Who pays for the free drink? The shop that pours it — and behind every earned freebie are six paid, stamped drinks, most poured at other counters. Boba comes four friends deep: the free drink walks in with three paying ones.
What does it cost? Pilot shops pour free. After that, a flat weekly fee — never a percentage of sales. Delivery apps take 25–30% of every order; this is one drink's cost of goods per seven visits.
Whose customers? Yours. Every stamp and redemption at your counter is visible to you, exportable, and never sold.
Setup? One QR sticker. No POS change, no hardware, no staff training, nothing to tap behind the counter.

What this is for: put it in front of five Burwood merchants and thirty students before writing a line of production code. If the aesthetic doesn't land here, it won't land at the counter — and that's a cheap thing to find out now.

Run a counter in Burwood, Eastwood or Haymarket? The first five pilot shops pour free — PEARL funds every launch freebie.
